GramAI Health Sector
====================

This is a static student prototype with no AI, scanner, chatbot, backend,
API key, or installation requirement.

To open:
1. Extract GramAI-Health-Guide.zip.
2. Open the GramAI-Health-Guide folder.
3. Double-click index.html in Chrome or Edge.

The site contains general public-health service information and links to
official Indian resources. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace care
from a qualified health professional. For immediate emergencies, call 112.

Official links used in this project:
- Emergency Response Support System: https://112.gov.in/
- Ayushman Arogya Mandir: https://aam.mohfw.gov.in/
- National Health Mission: https://nhm.gov.in/
