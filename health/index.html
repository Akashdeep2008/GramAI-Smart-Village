CARE THAT MEETS PEOPLE WHERE THEY ARE

Health is more than treatment.

Explore practical routes to prevention and support. A health professional can guide care for your personal situation.

01PREVENTION

Everyday wellbeing

Build routines around movement, sleep, balanced meals, hand hygiene and regular check-ins with a qualified provider.

Explore Ayushman Arogya Mandir ↗
02FAMILY HEALTH

Women, pregnancy & children

Ask an ASHA, ANM or local health centre about antenatal and postnatal visits, immunization sessions and child growth services.

National Health Mission resources ↗
03CHECK-UPS

Long-term conditions

Use a nearby primary care centre to ask about screening and follow-up for concerns such as blood pressure or blood sugar.

Find public primary-care information ↗
04MENTAL WELLBEING

Support is part of health

Speak with a qualified counsellor or health professional if stress, low mood or worry is affecting daily life. You deserve respectful support.

Ask a local health centre about support ↗
START WITH A TRUSTED LOCAL CONTACT

Care is closer than you think.

Ayushman Arogya Mandirs are intended to bring a broad range of primary health services closer to communities. Availability can vary locally; contact your village ASHA/ANM or nearest public health facility to confirm service hours and support.
