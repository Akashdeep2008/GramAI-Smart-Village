FROM OBSERVATION TO ACTION

A useful report
starts with good details.

Clear location and condition notes help the responsible local office understand what needs attention. Share observations respectfully and keep people safe.

01Describe what you seeSay what asset is affected and what changed.
02Give a clear locationUse a public landmark or road name; avoid private personal details.
03Use an official routeContact the Panchayat or relevant department; use an official portal where available.
04Keep a referenceNote the date and any complaint or follow-up number provided.
A SIMPLE REPORTING NOTE

Turn an observation into a clear note.

This tool prepares text on your device only. It does not submit a complaint or send information to GramAI. Use the note with your Panchayat, local utility or relevant department.

!

Do not include Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, private addresses or photos of people. Keep away from damaged wires, open drains and unsafe structures.

BUILT FOR THE LONG RUN

Infrastructure is more than construction.

Accessible — consider children, elders and people with disabilities.

Resilient — account for local terrain, monsoon and climate risks.

Maintainable — plan how assets will be inspected, repaired and funded.

Community-led — bring priorities to the Gram Sabha and responsible local bodies.
