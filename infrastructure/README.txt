GramAI Village Infrastructure Sector
====================================

This static student prototype covers village roads, streetlights and energy,
drainage, public buildings, sanitation and waste, and digital connectivity.
It has no AI, backend, API key, live data connection, or installation requirement.

To open:
1. Extract GramAI-Infrastructure-Guide.zip.
2. Open the GramAI-Infrastructure-Guide folder.
3. Double-click index.html in Chrome or Edge.

The village map is an illustration only. The issue-note builder keeps its draft
in the browser page and does not submit it anywhere. Use the linked official
service or contact the responsible Panchayat, utility or department.

Official links in the site include PMGSY / the National Government Services
Portal, eGramSwaraj / Ministry of Panchayati Raj, Swachh Bharat Mission-Gramin,
and BharatNet.
