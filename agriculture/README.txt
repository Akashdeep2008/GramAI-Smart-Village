GramAI Agriculture Crop Guides
==============================

This is a static student prototype containing 60 searchable crop-care guides.
It has no AI scanner, chatbot, backend, or API key requirement.

To open:
1. Extract GramAI-Agriculture-Guide.zip.
2. Open the GramAI-Agriculture-Guide folder.
3. Double-click index.html. Chrome or Edge will open the site.

Crop photos are fetched from Wikimedia Commons when an internet connection is
available. If a photo cannot load, an emoji placeholder remains visible.

The care notes are introductory and not a diagnosis or prescription. Watering
and nutrient decisions depend on local weather, soil, variety, and growth stage.
Confirm pest/disease identification and any treatment with a local Krishi Vigyan
Kendra (KVK), agriculture department, or qualified agriculture officer. Never
apply a pesticide or fertilizer dose based only on this student prototype.

Sources linked in the site:
- ICAR integrated pest management overview
- ICAR Kharif agro-advisories
