A SAFE WAY TO USE THIS GUIDE

Observe first.
Choose care carefully.

01

Check the field daily

Look at soil moisture, drainage, new growth and the underside of leaves. Note when a change began and whether it is spreading.

02

Water by need

Do not irrigate by a universal daily timer. Check the root zone and account for rain, soil, crop stage and local advice.

03

Confirm before treating

Similar symptoms can have different causes. Avoid guessing fertilizer or pesticide doses; consult a soil test and local KVK expert.

Guidance approach: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) integrated pest management emphasizes combining appropriate methods and avoiding unnecessary pesticide use. Read ICAR’s IPM overview ↗ · ICAR Kharif advisories ↗
